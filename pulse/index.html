The math

Which box this formula describes

Plain corrugated shipping box — single wall, 32 ECT, no print. Prices are quoted in dollars per thousand square feet of board (industry shorthand MSF — we spell it out on the calculator above).

Highway diesel — week of 28 Sep 2026

The index rose from 465.6 to 501.4 — about 7.7% higher. The 2026 value is still preliminary.

Cost mix

Roughly two-thirds of the price tracks paper (box index). About one-tenth tracks freight (highway diesel). The rest — converting, margin, other — stays flat in this model.

Steps (same logic as the calculator)

For diesel when you last bought, we use $3.73/gal (EIA highway diesel, late Aug 2025, alongside index 465.6). Swap in your week if you have it.

Split your old price

Paper: last × ⅔ · Freight: last × ⅒ · Other: the remainder (7/30 of last).

Move paper with the index

Paper slice × (501.4 ÷ 465.6).

Move freight with diesel

Freight slice × ($6.38 ÷ $3.73).

Add slices, then band

Fair = sum of updated slices. Low = fair × 0.92. High = fair × 1.08 (±8%).

Fair = last × [ ⅔×(501.4/465.6) + ⅒×(6.38/3.73) + 7/30 ]

Checkpoint: last price $90 → fair about $101, band about $93–$109 per thousand square feet.
