What should a plain corrugated box cost right now?

This page shows the math for one standard SKU so you can check a supplier quote without a briefing call. Every input below is public data or a number you already have from your last purchase.

Privacy: We do not keep your price. We do not have other buyers’ invoices. Nothing on this page asks you to submit a quote or payment.

The box we mean

Product
Plain shipping box — single wall, 32 ECT, no print
Price unit
Dollars per thousand square feet (MSF) of board

Public inputs (as of this write-up)

465.6
U.S. corrugated box index — Aug 2025
501.4
Same index — Aug 2026 (preliminary)
$6.38
Highway diesel — week of 28 Sep 2026

The index moved from 465.6 to 501.4 — about 7.7% higher. Treat the 2026 figure as preliminary until the government series is final.

What we assume about cost mix

For this plain box, we treat roughly two-thirds of the price as paper (it tracks the box index) and about one-tenth as freight (it tracks highway diesel). The remaining share covers converting, margin, and other fixed pieces — we leave that slice flat in this model.

Worked example — follow the steps

Suppose your last price was $90 per MSF, from when the index was 465.6. For diesel, use the EIA highway diesel price from that same period — here $3.73/gal (late Aug 2025), a public number you can swap for your own week if you prefer.

Split your old price into slices

Paper slice: $90 × ⅔ = $60.00
Freight slice: $90 × ⅒ = $9.00
Other (flat): $90 − $60 − $9 = $21.00

Update the paper slice with the index

Index ratio: 501.4 ÷ 465.6 = 1.077 (about +7.7%)
New paper: $60.00 × 1.077 = $64.62

Update the freight slice with diesel

Diesel ratio: $6.38 ÷ $3.73 = 1.711
New freight: $9.00 × 1.711 = $15.40 (rounds to the nearest cent in your spreadsheet)

Add the slices

Fair price ≈ $64.62 + $15.40 + $21.00 = $101.02 → about $101 per MSF.

Fair ≈ Old × [ ⅔×(501.4/465.6) + ⅒×(6.38/3.73) + 7/30×1 ]

Apply the fairness band (±8%)

Suppliers and mills rarely land on the exact cent. We use a ±8% band around the fair price:

Low: $101 × 0.92 ≈ $93
High: $101 × 1.08 ≈ $109

Your example, restated
Last price: $90 / MSF → fair price today: ~$101 / MSF
Reasonable quote band: about $93–$109 / MSF

How to use this on your own quote

  1. Confirm the SKU matches: plain, single wall, 32 ECT, no print.
  2. Plug in your last MSF price and the index value from that month.
  3. Use current index 501.4 (preliminary) and diesel $6.38 for the week of 28 Sep 2026.
  4. Run the same slice math, then apply ±8%.
  5. If a quote sits far outside the band, ask what changed — board grade, delivery distance, minimums, or a new surcharge — before you accept the increase.
